# Workspace

Open Workspace in the main navigation to see the files and connections Istota can reach for you. On a phone, open Places beside the page title to choose a location.

## Places and access

My files is your own workspace folder. Linked folders, room folders and group folders appear when they are available to your account. Room membership is checked again on each request, so leaving a room removes access to its folder.

Each place has a badge for a task in a private room: Read-write, Read-only, Via skill or No access. These badges describe task access; they do not mean that the web page can edit the files. Open “How Istota reaches your files” to see the selected place's access in a private room, on your own turn in a shared room, and on a guest's turn or a task nobody asked for. The disclosure also explains where files are stored and whether sandboxing limits task access.

Personal memory is not loaded into shared-room prompts, even when your own turn can read your files. A room folder is available to tasks in that room only. Connections such as Google Drive are reached through a skill during a task. Talk attachments show their reach but cannot be browsed here.

## Browsing

Choose a folder to open it. The breadcrumbs return to its parents, and the address bar keeps the current place and folder so browser Back, Forward and reload work. Files under My files open in the shared viewer. Other workspace locations can be browsed; opening their files is not yet available.

Hidden files are off by default. Show hidden remembers your choice in this browser. Symbolic links are labelled and cannot be opened or followed. Other special entries, such as sockets, are also inert.

A large directory shows at most 2,000 entries and a notice. This is a slice of the directory's enumeration order, sorted with folders first; it is not necessarily the alphabetically first 2,000 entries. Entries whose names cannot be represented as text are skipped and counted below the listing. A folder that cannot be read shows an error and a Retry button.
